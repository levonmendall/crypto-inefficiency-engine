# V3 Coverage Closure

V3 turns the V2.5 profit-mechanism taxonomy into active research infrastructure across every canonical economic mechanism while preserving the paper-only, fail-closed authority boundary.

## Active forward-tested alpha families

The shared Alpha Factory now contains six predictive families:

- time-series momentum;
- robust mean reversion;
- provider-gated on-chain/fundamental factors;
- cross-sectional relative value;
- L2 microstructure imbalance;
- provider-gated event-driven surprise.

Cross-sectional and microstructure alpha use market evidence already collected by the engine and therefore begin accumulating independent forward cohorts immediately. Event-driven and fundamental families have complete forward/statistical plumbing but cannot emit research signals until authoritative, commercially usable, point-in-time observations exist.

All predictive families share the same independent-outcome accounting, multiple-testing penalty, regime gate, fresh L2 execution economics, adaptive health controller, portfolio risk overlay, unified paper allocator, and supported allocation-forward certification.

## Generalized yield / staking / lending

V3 adds a provider-neutral yield contract covering staking, lending, fixed-yield, LP-fee and incentive income. Every observation includes capacity, holding period, entry/exit costs, lockups and explicit protocol/credit/slashing/liquidation/incentive-decay risk haircuts.

The engine can rank conservative net APY when authoritative evidence is supplied, but yield remains research-only until realized yield, exit liquidity and loss outcomes build an independent forward statistical record.

## Options / volatility

The volatility layer accepts point-in-time option quotes with bid/ask, IV and Greeks; groups near-ATM surfaces; and compares implied with realized volatility to identify volatility-risk-premium hypotheses. Bid/ask and hedge-cost evidence are explicit.

No option trade is paper allocatable yet. Authoritative option L2/capacity, forward delta-hedge economics, and higher-order risk validation remain required.

## Liquidity provision / market making

The market-making simulator models visible spread/depth, maker rebates, empirical fill probability, adverse selection, inventory penalties and queue-model quality. It refuses to call itself decision-grade when empirical queue position, maker fills or post-fill adverse selection are missing.

This prevents snapshot-based spread capture from being misreported as achievable market-making profit.

## Liquidation / solver / distress

Provider-neutral observations describe liquidation, solver-auction and backstop opportunities with capacity, gross reward, execution cost, worst-case recovery loss, capture probability and settlement probability. Conservative expected profit explicitly includes the failure state.

These opportunities remain research-only until capture/selection probabilities and recovery settlement are independently forward calibrated.

## Capital-location optimization

The capital-location research service learns where positive persisted opportunities historically occurred by venue and asset and produces constrained paper reserve recommendations. It has no authority to move balances or alter the unified allocator.

Forward incremental value, transfer/withdrawal latency and cost, and idle-capital opportunity cost must be validated before capital-location becomes an allocatable strategy.

## Coverage semantics

V3 deliberately separates **architecture closure** from **empirical closure**.

Building a provider-neutral contract and economic model advances a mechanism only to the stage justified by evidence. A mechanism becomes decision-grade only after authoritative data, explicit economics, independent forward evidence and a statistical gate all exist. The failure-conclusion gate therefore remains closed while any canonical mechanism lacks decision-grade evidence.

## Authority boundary

V3 remains paper-only. None of these research systems can access private balances, custody assets, sign transactions, submit orders, bypass promotion gates, or enable live execution.
